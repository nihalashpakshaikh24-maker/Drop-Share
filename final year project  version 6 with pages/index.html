How It Works

A quick walkthrough

Step 1

Enter your name

This is what your friend will see when they connect to you.

Step 2

Share your QR or link

Scan the QR code or copy the link and send it to your friend.

Step 3

Connect

Paste your friend's ID, or simply open the link — it auto-connects.

Step 4

Pick what to send

Choose Photo, Video, or File — your device's picker opens instantly.

Step 5

Watch it transfer

See live progress and transfer speed — all peer-to-peer.

Step 6

Received

The file downloads automatically on your friend's device.

Features

What makes DropShare special

⚡

Peer-to-Peer

Files go directly between devices. No server stores them.

🔒

End-to-End Encrypted

WebRTC uses DTLS-SRTP — nobody can intercept your files.

📱

QR & Link Sharing

Scan a QR or share a link — auto-connects instantly.

🗂️

Any File Type

Photos, videos, PDFs, ZIPs, APKs, EXE — anything works.

📊

Live Progress & Speed

Real-time progress bar with MB/s transfer speed display.

🌍

Works Anywhere

Same room or across the world — as long as both are online.

📦

Large File Support

Chunked streaming handles GB-scale files without memory issues.

🔄

Auto-Reconnect

Mobile tab switching doesn't break the connection.
